# Section 5: Automated Reputation & Review Generation Systems
## Organic Search Dominance
Over 70% of Google Local 3-Pack Map Box ranking weight is driven by Review Volume, Review Velocity, and Review Sentiment.

## Private Feedback Mitigation Gateway
Happy clients (4-5 stars) are guided directly to public Google Business Profile review links. Unhappy clients (1-3 stars) are routed to a private internal feedback form, alerting management privately before negative reviews hit Google.

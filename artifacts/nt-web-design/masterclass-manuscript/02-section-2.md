# Section 2: The 24/7 AI Lead Capture & Instant Missed-Call Text-Back Engine
## Executive Overview & The Zero-Latency Imperative
To eliminate structural funnel vulnerabilities, modern service enterprises must transition from passive web presences to an active, zero-latency lead ingestion engine.

## Technical Architecture of Missed-Call Text-Back
When an inbound call rings to your business line and goes unanswered, a telephony provider (Twilio API) fires an automated HTTP POST webhook payload within < 500ms. Within 8 seconds, an automated SMS text-back is dispatched to the prospect's mobile device.

## Conversational Qualification Trees
The AI system engages the prospect in a multi-turn dialog tree collecting Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline, and Location preferences (BANT-V Matrix), booking qualified leads directly into your calendar without human intervention.
